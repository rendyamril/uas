Things to See and Do in Jakarta

Rendy  •  Published by Passporter

1. Kota Tua, the historic centre of Jakarta

One of the must-see places in Jakarta is undoubtedly the historic city centre, known as Kota Tua. It is the area with the most tourist attractions in the city, where the Dutch influence is most noticeable on the facades of the buildings. In Kota Tua you can’t miss Fatahillah Square, the heart of the neighbourhood and the place to feel the essence of Jakarta. Here you’ll find some of the city’s most important museums, such as the Puppet Museum and the Jakarta History Museum. In addition, you can’t leave without renting one of the colourful vintage bikes and taking a ride around the square.

2. Istiqlal Mosque and Jakarta Cathedral

The Istiqlal Mosque is the third largest mosque in the world and one of the most important monuments to see in Jakarta. Its name means “independence” and it was built to commemorate it. It is a spectacular building, with a 45-metre dome supported by 12 columns and a single minaret, representing the divine unity of God. The Istiqlal Mosque is located in the centre of Jakarta, in front of the Merdeka Square and very close to the Cathedral. The Jakarta Cathedral is a Catholic monument built by the Dutch in the early 20th century. It is a beautiful neo-gothic building with two 60-metre high iron towers.

3. Monas, Indonesia’s National Monument

In the centre of Merdeka Square, the central square of Jakarta and one of the largest in the world, stands the Monas, the National Monument of Indonesia. It is a majestic 132-metre high tower covered in Italian marble and topped by a flame covered in gold leaf. The monument was built to represent the country’s struggle for independence. Inside there is a museum where you can learn about the history of Indonesia and you can also go up to the top of the tower, from where you can get the best views of Jakarta.

4. Ancol Beach

Ancol Beach is Jakarta’s only beach and a place where you can take a break from the hustle and bustle of the city and cool off from the heat. It’s a pleasant place to stroll along the promenade, but it’s not a paradise-like beach. It’s a very nice urban beach, with plenty of leisure options, such as renting boats to sail on its waters, or the Ancol Dream Park, an amusement park with different themed areas.

5. The Thousand Islands of Jakarta

Off the coast of Jakarta is a set of paradisiacal islands that are a real haven of peace where you can escape from the chaos of the city. They are the Thousand Islands of Jakarta, and although there are not a thousand of them, there are many islands where you can get lost and enjoy their beaches and nature. The best known are Bidadari, Onrust and Kelor, which are the closest to Jakarta and can be reached by ferry in about 30 minutes. In the Thousand Islands you can enjoy a pleasant day of sun and sea, as well as water activities such as snorkelling and diving, as they have a rich marine wildlife.
